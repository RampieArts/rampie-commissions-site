MODULE 03

FAQ

READOUT
01 How do I order?

Send me a Discord DM with the song, what you need (MIDI / tune / mix / video), length, and a deadline if you have one. We agree on the option and price before any work starts.

02 What if you don't have a specific voicebank?

If the voice bank requires a paid license, there are several options:

- I can work with the voice bank I already have and provide you with a project file for rendering.
- You can provide me with an account for the voice bank, if the engine technically allows it and that works for you.

I mostly work with Synthesizer V and Diffsinger, but I also have experience working with Vocaloid, UTAU, and Voisona.

03 What should I send for a cover?

MIDI, USTX, SVP, or VPR if you have it, any preferences - for example, how it should feel. Note the singer, tempo, and whether you want harmonies or backing vocals. WAV / instrumental helps for mix.

04 Are prices per finished minute?

The prices for covers listed on this page are per minute of track. Video prices are also per minute, but character animations are priced for the whole piece, since theese are looped animations and the price varies more depending on complexity.

05 How long does it take?

Depends on the option and the queue. A simple MIDI or lyric video is usually faster than a full cover or MV. I’ll give you a time window when we discuss.

06 Can I change something after delivery?

Small fixes and changes are part of the job. But bigger changes are treated as extra work, so I can ask for an extra.
